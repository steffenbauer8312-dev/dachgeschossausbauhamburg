6. **Image SEO is neglected.** Before/after galleries, proper alt text, and image schema are missing.
7. **FAQ sections are thin and generic.** No competitor has comprehensive, expert-quality FAQ content.
8. **No competitor targets entity clusters.** Everyone targets keywords, not knowledge graph-style entity relationships.

```
├── Genehmigung (/genehmigung) — legal/BayBO 2025 deep dive
├── Projekte (/projekte) — before/after gallery
├── Über Uns (/ueber-uns) — company, team, credentials
├── FAQ (/faq)
└── Kontakt (/kontakt) — lead form + location info
```

**Every page has a lead capture form in:**
- Fixed footer CTA bar (visible on scroll)
- Mid-content CTA blocks
- End-of-page CTA section

### Design Direction

- **Look and feel:** Clean, professional German craftsmanship. Warm but modern. Trust signals prominently displayed.
- **Typography:** Clear, readable. No overused startup fonts.
- **Color:** Dark slate/charcoal primary, warm amber/gold accent. Not the typical blue/purple AI aesthetic.
- **No stock photos of happy families.** Real project photography only — or none if unavailable.
- **No fake reviews.** Real testimonials only, with verifiable source if possible.

### Content Philosophy

1. **Entity SEO first.** Each page should be strongly connected to entities: location (München neighborhoods), legal concepts (BayBO Art. 57), services, processes. Use schema: LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, HowTo, BreadcrumbList, Organization.

2. **Semantic depth over length.** 1,500+ words on key pages, but every paragraph adds information. No filler. No "in today's world" filler phrases.

3. **Zero AI footprints.** We write like a German construction expert who has done this for 20 years, not like an AI. Short sentences. Direct claims. No hedging. Real data.

4. **Cost transparency.** This is what German homeowners want — real numbers, real ranges, no "call for quote" evasion.

5. **Authority signals.** Proper imprint, real company registration, Handwerkskammer membership if applicable, explicit credentials.
