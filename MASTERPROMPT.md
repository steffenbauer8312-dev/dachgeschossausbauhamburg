## CONTENT REQUIREMENTS

### Style Guide
- **Voice:** Expert German craftsman. Direct, factual, no hedging.
- **Sentence length:** Short. Max 20 words per sentence.
- **Paragraphs:** 2-3 sentences max. No walls of text.
- **Filler phrases to AVOID:** "In der heutigen Zeit", "Es ist wichtig zu beachten", "Wie Sie sicherlich wissen", "lassen Sie uns zunächst..."
- **Never use:** "Unser Unternehmen bietet...", "Wir sind stolz darauf...", "Als führender Anbieter..."
- **Always use:** Real data, specific ranges, named regulations, concrete examples

### Topics to Cover Deeply

**Kosten Page (Critical):**
- Cost per m² range for Munich (€1,800–3,800/m² depending on standard)
- Gaube costs: Schleppgaube €10,000–18,000, Frontgaube €25,000+
- Permit costs: €250–400 for genehmigung
- Statik costs: what affects structural work pricing
- Comparison: Ausbau mit vs. ohne Gaube
- Financing options available
- Timeline cost implications

**Genehmigung Page (Major Opportunity):**
- BayBO 2025 Änderungen (Art. 57) — verfahrensfrei rules

- Two-week notice requirement to Stadt München
- When you still need full Baugenehmigung
- Stellplatzpflicht in München (new rules 2025)
- Common mistakes that delay projects
- Step-by-step approval timeline

**Prozess Page:**
- Initial consultation and on-site assessment
- Planning phase (what to expect, timeline)
